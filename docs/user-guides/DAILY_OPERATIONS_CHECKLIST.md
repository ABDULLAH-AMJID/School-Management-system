# Daily Operations Checklist

## Opening

- Host PC, UPS, router and switch are operating.
- ERP opens from one desktop and one phone on the LAN.
- `php artisan school:health` has no required-system failures.
- Queue worker and scheduler are running.
- WhatsApp status is connected or the responsible person is informed.

## During the day

- Student and staff attendance submitted.
- Fee receipts checked before handing to parents.
- Approval inbox reviewed by authorized staff.
- Failed WhatsApp messages investigated without uncontrolled repeated sending.
- Staff use separate accounts and lock unattended devices.

## Closing

- Cash collection agrees with receipts.
- Pending finance exceptions documented.
- Encrypted backup completed.
- Backup copied to separate media according to policy.
- Host remains on for scheduled work, or the approved shutdown process is followed.
