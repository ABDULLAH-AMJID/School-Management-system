# Teacher Guide

## First login

Sign in with your personal account and immediately replace the temporary password. Do not share credentials. Your menu displays only authorized modules.

## Attendance

Choose the section and date, mark each student present, absent, late or on leave, then submit. Verify the success message. Absence communication follows the school's configured approval and consent policy; avoid repeatedly resubmitting an unchanged register.

## Marks and results

Select the correct examination component and class. Enter marks only within the displayed maximum. Use absent or exempt status when appropriate. Recheck entries before the principal publishes results; published examinations cannot be edited through normal marks entry.

## Timetable and students

Use the timetable for assigned classes and periods. Report conflicts to the principal rather than creating informal changes. Student records contain sensitive information and must only be used for school work.

## Leave

Submit leave from HR with correct dates and a clear reason. The decision can be communicated to your registered phone after approval.
